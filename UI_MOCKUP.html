Click the screen, then use your keyboard (F1–F10, arrows, Enter, Tab, Esc)

Keys: F2 menu · F3 view · F4 append · F5 info · F6 rename · F7 new · F8 delete · F9 refresh · F10 quit · Tab switch pane · Enter open · Backspace parent folder. You can also click the key bar and the file rows. Press Esc to close a dialog.
